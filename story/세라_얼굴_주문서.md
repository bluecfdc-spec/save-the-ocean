# 세라 얼굴 시안 주문서 (GPT 그림 프로젝트용)

> 2026-10-15 · 제네시스 본부. 목표: **"양산형 AI 미녀" 얼굴을 버리고, 정이 가는 특이한 얼굴**로 세라를 다시 잡는다.
> 사용법: GPT 그림 프로젝트에 아래 **[공통 지시]**를 먼저 붙이고, **[시안 A] → [B] → [C]**를 한 번에 하나씩 요청한다.
> 첨부: ① `story/sera_master.jpg`(옷·창 참고용 — **얼굴은 따라 하지 말 것**) ② 탱크럼블 게임 그림 1장(화풍 기준).
> 뽑은 결과는 PC `Genesis\Story\Img` 폴더에 `sera_face_A.png` / `B` / `C` 로 저장 → 본부 채팅에 "세라 시안 나왔어".

---

## [공통 지시] — 매번 맨 앞에 붙이기

```
You are designing the face of SERA, the heroine of a mobile game universe called DIVINE GENESIS.
She is a brilliant AI researcher in her early 30s who one day finds a glowing spear in her hand.

STYLE: premium stylized cartoon 3D, like Clash Royale / Boom Beach character art.
Match the rendering, outline and lighting of the attached game asset. NOT semi-realistic, NOT anime, NOT photo.

THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: she must NOT look like a generic AI beauty.
Players should remember her face after one look and feel affection for her — the way people love a quirky, imperfect movie heroine.
Give her clearly asymmetric, characterful features. Slight imperfections are good.

DO NOT: idol face, K-pop makeup, V-line jaw, doll face, huge sparkly eyes, flawless skin, glamour pose,
heavy lipstick, perfect symmetry, "beautiful/gorgeous" look, sexualized pose or outfit.

KEEP from the attached master sheet (outfit only): dark navy officer long coat with silver trim, black turtleneck,
black gloves, low ponytail of black hair. The face must be NEW — do not copy the master's face.

OUTPUT: one sheet, solid flat green background (#00B140), no text.
Top row: three head shots of the SAME face — neutral / laughing / angry.
Bottom: one full-body shot standing, holding the spear upright.
```

## ★ [시안 D] — 사진 바탕 (2026-10-15 홍균 결정, 1순위)

> 실제 인물 사진을 바탕으로 한다(본인 동의 받음). **사진은 공개 저장소에 올리지 않고 GPT에 직접 첨부만** 한다.
> [공통 지시]를 붙인 뒤 사진을 첨부하고 아래를 붙인다. D1·D2·D3를 하나씩 따로 요청.

```
Face design D — based on the attached PHOTO (the person agreed).
Turn this real face into SERA in the premium stylized cartoon 3D game style. Keep her recognizable,
but stylized — not a photo, not a filter. Keep the warm, approachable feeling of the photo.

Keep from the photo:
- Soft round-oval face with gently full cheeks.
- Straight, slightly see-through bangs covering the forehead; long straight black hair (tie it in a LOW PONYTAIL for battle, bangs stay).
- Gentle, slightly downturned round eyes with soft under-eye puffiness (aegyo-sal) — kind, calm gaze.
- Small soft nose with a rounded tip.
- Small mouth with a quiet closed-lip smile that lifts a little more on one side.
Do NOT turn her into an idol: no bigger eyes, no sharper V-line chin, no heavier makeup than the photo, no slimming.
Her strength should come from her expression and posture, not from changing her face.
```

**D1 — 그대로**: 위 설명만. (사진 인상에 가장 가깝게)

**D2 — 각성의 흔적 하나**: 위 설명 끝에 덧붙이기
```
Add ONE signature mark: a single thin streak of white hair in her bangs, which appeared the day the spear came to her.
```

**D3 — 전장의 세라**: 위 설명 끝에 덧붙이기
```
Show her after battle: a small adhesive bandage on one cheek, a few loose strands of hair, a determined look
— but the same gentle eyes. Her laugh face should still be warm and a little shy.
```

## [시안 A] — "웃으면 무너지는 천재"

```
Face design A:
- Long face with a slightly too-long nose and a small bump on the bridge.
- Thick, straight eyebrows; the LEFT eyebrow is split by a thin old scar.
- Narrow, sleepy-looking eyes with heavy lids — she always looks a little tired from lab nights.
- A visible gap between her front teeth when she laughs; her laugh is big and crooked (one side of the mouth higher).
- Light freckles across the nose and cheeks.
- A pencil stuck behind her right ear.
Personality to show: blunt and serious at work, but when she laughs her whole face falls apart.
```

## [시안 B] — "흰 머리 한 가닥의 각성자"

```
Face design B:
- Round face with full cheeks and a small, upturned button nose.
- Wide-set eyes (big distance between them), slightly different in size.
- One single streak of WHITE hair in her black bangs — it appeared the day the spear came to her.
- A small mole under her right eye.
- Short, stubby eyebrows that move a lot — very expressive.
- Ears that stick out a little; she hides them under her hair.
Personality to show: looks gentle and a bit clumsy, but her eyes turn fierce in battle.
```

## [시안 C] — "반창고를 달고 사는 사람"

```
Face design C:
- Square jaw and strong chin, a bit masculine and sturdy.
- Low, dark, almost straight-line eyebrows that make her look stern.
- When she smiles her eyes become thin crescent moons and her cheeks push up — a total contrast to her stern face.
- A small adhesive bandage on her nose bridge (she always has one somewhere).
- Slightly crooked front tooth.
- A thin silver earpiece/hearing-aid-like lab device on her left ear.
Personality to show: tough and stubborn, secretly soft.
```

## 고를 때 기준 (홍균)

- 3초 보고 **얼굴이 기억나는가**
- 탱크·블록 그림 옆에 두었을 때 **한 게임처럼 보이는가**
- 화난 얼굴과 웃는 얼굴이 **같은 사람으로 보이는가**
- 마음에 드는 부분만 섞어도 됩니다(예: "A 얼굴에 B의 흰 머리") → 본부에 말하면 섞은 주문서를 다시 써 드림.
